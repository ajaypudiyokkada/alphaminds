The Alpha Framework

Five pillars that shape the complete athlete

Personality Assessment & Development

Know the athlete before shaping the athlete
  • Mental Toughness Assessment
  • MBTI-based personality insight

  • Short- and long-term performance goals

Cognitive Performance

Sharper thinking. Faster reactions. Better decisions.
  • Cognitive vision assessment & training
  • Cognitive HIIT
  • Focus and attention training
  • Working memory, reaction time, planning & predictability

Emotional Regulation

Calm under pressure is a trainable skill
  • Individualized breathing protocols
  • Heart-specific breathing
  • Guided meditation
  • Visualization and mental imagery

Adaptability & Resilience

Train the brain to adjust, recover, and respond
  • Neuroplasticity-driven activities
  • Intuition versus logic training
  • Right- versus left-brain stimulation
  • Physical balance, new skills, and random challenges

Eco Social Connectedness

Great athletes are deeply connected humans
  • Real-life scenario training
  • Society engagement
  • Nature immersion: trekking and camping
  • Friendship, belonging, and social confidence
